A Windows desktop app for Google Calendar and Google Tasks

Your calendar at a glance.
Today’s tasks, done lightly.

Desk Calendar puts your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks side by side in one clean window on your Windows PC. See the whole month, catch upcoming deadlines and add what’s next without opening a browser.

Features

Everything for today, in one window

Desk Calendar shows the events and tasks from your own Google account, and lets you change them right where you see them.

Calendar and tasks together

Month, week and day views with a mini calendar. Google Calendar events and Google Tasks appear side by side, and the upcoming list counts down to each deadline (D-2, D-8 …).

Edit without a browser

Add, edit, duplicate and delete events and tasks in the app. Drag an item to another day, or drag its bottom edge in the week and day views to change how long it runs. Tick a checkbox to complete a task.

Monthly recurring tasks

Repeat a task on a set day of every month or on a weekday such as the third Wednesday, for 1 to 60 months. Desk Calendar creates one Google task for each month, and you can change just one or all the ones that follow.

More than one Google account

Add your work and personal Google accounts and switch between them from the account card.

Keeps working offline

If your internet connection drops, the events and tasks you last synced stay on screen.

Light, dark and always at hand

Choose a light or dark theme. Desk Calendar can start with Windows and waits in the system tray when you close its window.

Your Google data

How Desk Calendar uses your Google data

Desk Calendar asks for access to your Google account only to show and edit your own calendar and tasks. The app runs on your PC and talks directly to Google. There is no Desk Calendar server, so your data never reaches us.

Google Calendar

auth/calendar

To show your calendars and events, and to create, change, move or delete an event when you do so in the app.

Google Tasks

auth/tasks

To show your task lists and tasks, and to create, edit, complete or delete a task when you do so in the app, including the monthly recurring tasks you set up.

Your name and email address

openid · email · profile

To show which Google account you are signed in with and to tell your accounts apart.

Stays on your PC

  • Your sign-in token, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI)
  • An offline copy of your events and tasks
  • Your settings

What never happens

  • Your data is sent to us or to anyone other than Google
  • Your data is sold or used for advertising
  • Your data is used to train AI or machine-learning models
  • Analytics or tracking inside the app

You’re in control

  • Signing out deletes the token and offline copy from your PC
  • Remove the app’s access at any time in your Google Account
  • No Google account? Use local mode. Everything stays on your PC

Desk Calendar’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The details are in our Privacy Policy.

Download

Get Desk Calendar

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • .NET 11 Desktop Runtime. If you don’t have it, install it from Microsoft or run: 
    winget install Microsoft.DotNet.DesktopRuntime.11
  • A Google account, if you want to sync with Google Calendar and Google Tasks
  • The app’s interface is in Korean

Install and sign in

  1. Download the latest version from GitHub Releases.
  2. Extract the zip file to a folder and run desk-calendar.exe.
  3. Click Google 로그인 (Sign in with Google). Your browser opens Google’s sign-in page, then brings you back to the app. Desk Calendar never sees your password.
