Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

In short

  • Desk Calendar runs on your PC and connects only to Google. We do not run a server and never receive your data.
  • Your Google data is used only to show and edit your own calendar and tasks in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Signing out deletes your sign-in token and the offline copy of your data from your PC.

1. Who we are

Desk Calendar (“the app”) is a Windows desktop application made by Desk Calendar Team (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, stored and protected, and the choices you have. It also covers this website.

2. Google user data the app accesses

The app asks for the permissions below only when you click Google 로그인 (Sign in with Google) and agree on Google’s consent screen. If you don’t sign in, the app runs in local mode and accesses no Google data.

3. How the app uses Google user data

The app uses Google user data only to provide the features you see in the app:

  • showing your calendars, events, task lists and tasks;
  • creating, changing, moving, completing or deleting events and tasks when you ask it to;
  • creating one task per month for a monthly recurring task you set up;
  • showing the signed-in account and switching between accounts.

The app does not use your data for any other purpose.

4. Where your data is stored

Everything the app stores stays on your PC, in the folder %APPDATA%\com.desktop.deskcalendardock:

  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) so that only your Windows user account can read them;
  • an offline copy of your calendars, events, task lists and tasks, so you can see them when you are offline;
  • your settings, the list of accounts you added, and the details of monthly recurring tasks you set up.

The app exchanges data only with Google’s servers, over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in uses Google’s OAuth 2.0 with PKCE in your web browser, so the app never sees your Google password.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transfer or share your Google user data with anyone. Your data moves only between your PC and Google, and it never reaches us. In particular:

  • we do not use or transfer your data for advertising, including personalized or retargeted ads;
  • we do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending;
  • no person, including us, reads your data;
  • we do not use your data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Desk Calendar’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

The data on your PC stays there until you remove it:

  • Sign out (account card → 로그아웃) removes the account from the app and deletes its sign-in token and offline copy from your PC.
  • To delete everything, quit the app and delete the folder %APPDATA%\com.desktop.deskcalendardock.

Signing out does not cancel the permission you gave on Google’s side. To do that, open Third-party apps & services in your Google Account and remove Desk Calendar.

Your events and tasks themselves are kept in your Google account. Removing the app does not delete them. They are covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Using the app without a Google account

If you don’t sign in, the events and tasks you create are saved only on your PC, in the same folder, and are never sent anywhere.

8. This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. As with any website, the hosting service (GitHub) and the font service (jsDelivr) receive technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact Desk Calendar Team at milionesmio@gmail.com.
